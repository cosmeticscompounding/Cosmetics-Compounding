Custom Pharmaceutical Compounding

One of the most important services offered by Cosmetics Compounding is custom pharmaceutical compounding.

Pharmaceutical compounding is the process of preparing personalized medications or skincare formulations specifically for an individual's needs. Unlike commercially manufactured medicines, compounded preparations allow pharmacists to modify ingredients, strengths, dosage forms, flavors, or excipients to better suit patients when standard products are unsuitable.

Compounded preparations may include:

Dermatological creams
Topical gels
Ointments
Oral preparations
Rectal preparations
Customized cosmetic formulations
Specialty skincare compounds

Compounding is particularly valuable for individuals requiring customized concentrations, allergy-friendly ingredients, or formulations unavailable through commercial manufacturers.

Niacinamide Serum

Niacinamide, commonly known as Vitamin B3, is one of the most researched ingredients in modern skincare.

It supports multiple biological processes within the skin including:

Strengthening the skin barrier
Reducing excessive oil production
Improving uneven skin tone
Minimizing the appearance of enlarged pores
Supporting hydration
Reducing redness
Improving skin texture
Helping reduce post-inflammatory pigmentation

Because niacinamide is generally well tolerated by most skin types, it is widely incorporated into professional skincare formulations designed for acne-prone, oily, combination, and aging skin.

TCA Chemical Peel

A Trichloroacetic Acid (TCA) Chemical Peel is a professional skin resurfacing procedure that uses controlled chemical exfoliation to remove damaged outer skin layers and encourage healthier skin renewal.

Depending on concentration, TCA peels may help improve:

Acne scars
Fine lines
Sun damage
Uneven pigmentation
Melasma
Rough skin texture
Photoaged skin

As damaged skin is removed, healthier skin gradually replaces older tissue, improving overall appearance and texture.

Because chemical peels involve controlled skin injury, proper patient selection, application technique, and aftercare are essential.

Monobenzone Cream

Monobenzone is a depigmenting agent primarily used for patients with extensive vitiligo.

Unlike conventional skin-lightening ingredients, Monobenzone permanently removes remaining normal pigmentation to create a more uniform skin appearance when depigmentation has already become widespread.

Important considerations include:

Permanent depigmentation
Medical supervision recommended
Not intended for cosmetic whitening
Typically reserved for extensive vitiligo
Requires careful patient counseling

Its mechanism differs significantly from common brightening agents such as kojic acid, arbutin, niacinamide, or Vitamin C because it destroys pigment-producing melanocytes rather than temporarily reducing melanin production.

Further information regarding the Monobenzone Cream is available at https://cosmeticscompounding.com/products/monobenzone-cream.
